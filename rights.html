Page 2.0 · Worker rights

Understand what you are agreeing to.

Read the contract carefully before you accept an overseas job. This is general awareness, not legal advice.

Written contract

Job title, duties, employer and work place. Keep a copy.

Salary and deductions

Salary, payment dates, deductions and any fees.

Working conditions

Housing, food, working hours, rest days and leave.

Travel documents

Visa, passport handling and return travel.

Contract checklist

Who is the employer?

Full name and address, written in the contract.

What exactly is the job?

Job title and duties. Be careful if they keep changing.

How and when will I be paid?

Amount, currency and payment date.

Who pays for the visa, flight and fees?

It should be written down before you pay anything.

How can the contract end?

Ask how you or the employer can end it, and who pays for the return ticket.
